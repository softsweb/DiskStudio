Calibrate another disc

  1. Pick the closest preset and generate the calibration test disc.
  2. Burn it, photograph it face-on and fit it in the Windows app, Calibration workspace.
  3. Import the exported .calibration.json here, or type the numbers under Edit calibration and save a preset.
Your presets are saved in webui/presets.user.json. The same id overrides a built-in one.

Disc Studio is a local interface made by SoftsWeb. It runs on your machine and sends nothing anywhere. The disc engine is Disc Image Studio by JiaFeiMiao-K-Cat, GPL-3.0.
